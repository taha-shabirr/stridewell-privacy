Stridewell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Who we are

Stridewell keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We have no accounts, no servers and no way to see your data.

Stridewell is a fitness, food and habit coaching app for Android, made by Taha Shabbir, Muzaffargarh, Pakistan ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where it stays and what you can do about it. It applies to the Stridewell app (package com.stridewell.app) from version 0.1.0.

What the app stores

The app only stores what you type in, choose, or record with its tools. All of it stays on your phone.

The app does not collect your contacts, files, advertising ID, payment details or browsing history.

Where your data is kept

Everything is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • No account. You never sign up, so there is no profile on any server.
  • No servers. The app does not send your profile, health answers, logs or photos to us or to anyone else.
  • No cloud backup. Android's automatic Google backup is turned off for Stridewell, so your health data and photos are not copied to the cloud.
  • Your own backup. Settings has a text backup you can copy and keep yourself, for example in a note or a message to yourself. Progress photos are not included in it.

No internet access. Stridewell does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Links you tap (for example an exercise video search) open in your browser, outside the app.

Each permission is used only for the feature named below. You can turn any of them off in Android Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Food labels are read on your phone, and the label photo is deleted straight after. Voice typing is done by your phone's speech service (for example Google), which may process your voice under its own privacy policy. Type instead if you prefer that nothing you say leaves the phone.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools in the app.

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it yourself:

  • Text backup, which you copy and save wherever you like
  • Health report, which you copy and share with your doctor
  • Summaries, which you copy and paste into WhatsApp or anywhere else
  • Progress card, a screen you can screenshot and post or send

Once you send something, the app or person you sent it to handles it under their own rules.

Children and teens

Stridewell is for people aged 13 and over. If you enter a birth year that makes you under 13, the app will not continue setup.

For people aged 13 to 17, the app:

  • shows no calorie numbers or calorie targets, only food groups, protein and water
  • does not offer progress photos

We recommend that teens use the app with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Health notice

Stridewell gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting exercise if you are pregnant, have a heart, blood pressure, sugar or joint condition, or feel chest pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Stop and get medical help if you feel unwell during exercise.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your phone, you are in full control:

  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → Stridewell → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete photos: remove them on the Progress photos page, or turn photos off.

We hold no copy, so once it is deleted it cannot be recovered unless you kept your own backup.
